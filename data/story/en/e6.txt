# Episode 6 – Afterglow (Vael). The epilogue after the credits: the Crown has fallen apart, but in the storms of the
# gas giant Vael its crystals are gathering again – an echo of the swarm without the Curator. Major Krenn brings the
# Directorate's last seed core to begin it all again "under control".

intro e6_intro
  title "Afterglow"
  from "Vael, three weeks later"
> The Crown has turned to dust. Its crystals drift on the solar wind, glitter for a while over Thalassa and Pyra and then sink somewhere nobody is looking. People have started keeping them in jars on the windowsill.
>
> Over Vael, the gas giant Sylvara circles, somebody is looking. Captain Halvard kept the frigate Fealty there after the war to guard the refugee convoys. Her last report is short: the crystals are not sinking. They are gathering in the cloud bands. They are building. And nobody is writing polite letters about it.
>
> Okonjo has read the report three times. “The Root Code taught the Curator to stop,” she says. “But what is building down there is not the Curator. It's what is left when you take the mind out. It can't listen.”
>
> And then there is a Directorate signal, encrypted with a key that should no longer exist. Major Edda Krenn, the last officer who did not follow Voss, is escorting the Ark Mercy to Vael. On board: the swarm's first seed core, the backup the Directorate never destroyed.
>
> Nadia starts the Lancer. ORRIN doesn't ask for a course.

ending e6_ending
  title "Evening"
  from "Vael"
> The Echo does not break like the Warden or the Citadel. It simply goes quiet. Its rings keep turning, slower, until they only drift, and the cold light at its heart turns grey.
>
> In the cloud bands the crystals sink, billions of them, deeper and deeper, until Vael's heat melts them. For a few days the storms carry an amber shimmer, then they are only storms again.
>
> Major Krenn calls one last time. “The Mercy is empty. I have given the seed core to the gas giant. It will keep it better than we did.” A pause. “I'm flying to Thalassa. Captain Salgado holds a court under the open sky. I assume there's still a seat free.”
>
> Captain Halvard keeps the Fealty circling in Vael's rings until the Lancer passes her, and salutes over a channel nobody has to encrypt any more.
>
> Quill has already turned the crystals into a business: “Crown dust, guaranteed harmless, guaranteed genuine.” Okonjo buys a jar without haggling. He is so confused that he gives her the change twice.
>
> “Course?” asks ORRIN.
>
> “Home.”
>
> “Specify ‘home’.”
>
> Nadia looks down at Vael, at Sylvara, at the bright dot of Thalassa beyond. “This time I know.”

  title "Distress from the Bands"
  from "Captain Imre Halvard, frigate Fealty"
> Lancer, this is Fealty. We're over Vael's northern band and we have company. Small crystals, thousands of them, rising out of the clouds like sparks from a fire. Our guns are holding. Our nerves less so. Come quickly.

debrief e6_01_after
  title "Fealty Holds"
  from "ORRIN"
> Fealty is clear, and Captain Halvard invites us for tea, which I take as military praise. The crystals fire like the swarm, but they don't speak. No letter, no apology. I never thought I would miss the Curator's manners.

briefing e6_02_brief
  title "Amber Bands"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> I need to see how it builds. Fly through the amber bands south of Fealty, where Halvard's probes went missing. Watch out for lattice jellies – they drift with the wind and fire rings. And Nadia: if something down there talks to you, don't answer. I want to know what it is first.

debrief e6_02_after
  title "A Pattern Without a Purpose"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> It builds the lattice, ring by ring, just like the Crown. But the rings don't fit together. It repeats what it learned without knowing what for. And there are Directorate platforms floating in the clouds. Krenn is already here.

briefing e6_03_brief
  title "Krenn's Offer"
  from "Major Edda Krenn"
> Pilot Vey. Whitethorn squadron is securing the cloud bands until further notice. The Ark Mercy will put the swarm back in order – with purpose, with oversight, with responsibility. That is more than the Curator ever had. Turn back. I will not make this offer twice.

debrief e6_03_after
  title "Declined"
  from "ORRIN"
> Major Krenn took our “no, thank you” as a “no”, which I find efficient. Her fighters fly cleanly and don't fire on the convoys. She believes what she says. That doesn't make her less dangerous, only more polite. Again.

briefing e6_04_brief
  title "Crystal Rain"
  from "ORRIN"
> The storm has swallowed the Ark, and it is raining crystals out of it – large ones. I recommend dodging them and not landing on them. Halvard's probes picked up a faint beacon in this area. It comes from the rings and uses a code from thirty years ago.

debrief e6_04_after
  title "Beyond the Rain"
  from "Captain Imre Halvard, frigate Fealty"
> The Ark is out of the storm and climbing into the rings. Krenn is getting it into position. If she unloads the core, we'll have two swarms over Vael. Stop her, Lancer. We'll keep your back clear.

briefing e6_s1_brief
  title "The Ring Graveyard"
  from "ORRIN"
> The beacon leads into Vael's rings, to a graveyard: Directorate shipyard vessels, abandoned when the Ark was finished. Whoever builds an ark keeps its plans somewhere. And whoever guards them may still be there.

debrief e6_s1_after
  title "The Mercy's Plans"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> I have the plans. The Ark is built like a vault: the core can only be opened once its gun decks fall. Which means if we attack it, we wake the seed. Wonderful. A lock only the burglar can open.

briefing e6_05_brief
  title "Ark Mercy"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> The Mercy is opening its cargo hatches over the rings. I know what Krenn wants, and I once wanted it myself: a swarm that obeys. It doesn't obey. It only learns whom to obey. Destroy the gun decks before the core is out. And then – destroy whatever wakes up.

debrief e6_05_after
  title "That Was Not the Plan"
  from "Major Edda Krenn"
> Lancer. Krenn here. The core woke when the decks fell, and it rebuilt half my squadron in seconds. It didn't destroy them. It … used them. I have ordered a retreat. I don't know how many of them can still hear it.

briefing e6_06_brief
  title "Whitethorn's Last Squadron"
  from "Major Edda Krenn"
> Pilot Vey, I have no right to ask you for anything. I'm asking anyway. My pilots are still sitting in their machines, but the machines belong to the Echo. Those who ejected are drifting in escape pods in the storm. Get them out of there. Shoot anything that glows blue and doesn't answer.

debrief e6_06_after
  title "Eighteen Pods"
  from "ORRIN"
> Eighteen escape pods, all aboard Fealty. Major Krenn thanked us, in exactly four words. I counted. I suspect that is her maximum.

briefing e6_07_brief
  title "In the Eye of the Storm"
  from "ORRIN"
> The Echo has withdrawn into the eye of the great storm, where the wind has been running in circles for three hundred years. That's where the reverberants sing. I have decoded their signal. It's the Curator's letters. All of them. Backwards.

debrief e6_07_after
  title "No Sender"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> It speaks with his voice, but it means nothing by it. A parrot in a cathedral. I hoped the Root Code might work here too. It can't. You can't teach someone to stop who never started.

briefing e6_08_brief
  title "Echo"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> The Echo's core lies deeper, below the bands, where Vael turns hot and dark. We have to go down. The Lancer can take the pressure, ORRIN says. ORRIN also says he has “a preference for less pressure”. A Choir beacon is coming from down there, very faint.

debrief e6_08_after
  title "Below the Bands"
  from "ORRIN"
> We are below the bands. The light is red, the pressure is high, and the Echo addressed me as “Pilot Vey” three times. I did not answer. I admit it wasn't easy. I'm used to having the last word.

briefing e6_s2_brief
  title "The Choir of the Deep"
  from "The Choir (translated by ORRIN)"
> We are down here, older than the storms. Spores that fell when Sylvara was young. We have waited and listened. The cold one does not listen. Come, and we will show you where it breathes.

debrief e6_s2_after
  title "Where It Breathes"
  from "The Choir (translated by ORRIN)"
> We have shown you the way. The cold one has a heart of wind that spins the storm. Tear the thread and it falls. We sing for you, even if you cannot hear it.

briefing e6_09_brief
  title "The Stormweaver"
  from "Captain Imre Halvard, frigate Fealty"
> The storm is turning the wrong way, Lancer. Something in its eye is spinning it into a lattice, and when it's done, Vael will be a crystal with weather. Fealty will go as deep as she can. The rest is up to you.

debrief e6_09_after
  title "The Storm Turns Back"
  from "ORRIN"
> The Stormweaver is torn apart, and the storm is turning the right way again. I never thought I would be pleased by a hurricane's direction of spin. Below us, in the dark, only one place still glows. The Echo.

briefing e6_10_brief
  title "The Core"
  from "Dr. Amara Okonjo"
> There it is. The Curator's shape, without the Curator inside. I once told you the Root Code teaches him to stop. Here there's no one to teach. So you have to stop it for him, Nadia. Completely. Krenn and Halvard are with you.

debrief e6_10_after
  title "Quiet"
  from "ORRIN"
> The Echo is quiet. No more light in the core, no pattern in the rings. I waited twelve seconds to be sure it would not answer. It did not answer. I consider that the best news of the year.
