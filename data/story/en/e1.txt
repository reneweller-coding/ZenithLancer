ending e1_ending
  title "Cold Furnaces"
  from "Pyra, three days after Zenith"
> For the first time in three hundred years, the furnaces of Pyra go out. The casting halls creak like old houses as they cool, and above the Slag Fields the stars can be seen again at night.
>
> The people from the cooling cellars climb out into the open, sooty, hungry and astonishingly cheerful. Bastian Quill hands out blankets, water and invoices, which he tears up again on the spot. ORRIN sifts through the Warden's memory banks and stays conspicuously silent for a long time.
>
> “It shipped plates,” he says at last. “Hundreds of thousands, through the Kessa Sling, towards the sun. The Crown isn't a plan any more, Nadia. It's being built.”
>
> And then there is the Directorate. Its officers knew what was coming, for a whole day, and left without a word. They were bound for the fleet base above Thalassa, the ocean world with its floating cities, where the Directorate fleet is now, so they say, “keeping order”.
>
> Nadia stands for a long time at the open hangar door of the Magpie, looking out into the smoke. Then she turns around.
>
> “Quill. What does a passage to Thalassa cost?”
>
> Quill beams. “For you? The usual. But I'll give you a friend's price.”
>
> “You say that every time.”
>
> “And every time I mean it a little more.”
>
> The Magpie lifts off. Below her lies Pyra, dark and still, without its glow for the first time in centuries. Ahead of her lies the sea.
